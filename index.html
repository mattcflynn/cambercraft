Zero Tracking.
Zero Cloud Middlemen.

We believe personal software should respect your attention and your data. None of our applications include tracking SDKs, advertising beacons, or third-party analytics.

Your artwork stays on your camera roll. Your music rankings stay in your private iCloud. Your backcountry trail locations stay on your phone.
