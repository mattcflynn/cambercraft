StudioScan

A scanner for drawings, paintings and comics up to letter size. It corrects the camera, never the artwork, and tells you every change it made.

Why not just use the camera?

Your camera app is built to make photos look good. It sharpens, brightens the shadows and blends several frames. That flatters a snapshot and changes a copy. StudioScan does the opposite. It corrects what the camera and the light got wrong, leaves the artwork alone, and lists every change it made.

What it does

  • Helps you take the shot. A level shows when the phone is parallel to the work, the shutter waits until your hand is steady, and the app warns you when the light is uneven.
  • Crops and straightens. It finds the edges of the artwork and squares it up.
  • Fixes the camera, not the art. White is set from the paper and the lens's dark corners are removed. No sharpening, no filters, no AI.
  • Records the real size. Type the width of the piece and the file carries its true DPI, so it opens and prints at the size of the original.
  • Scans pages. Shoot a whole comic or sketchbook in one session and export it as a PDF, a CBZ for comic readers, or a multi-page TIFF.

The files

  • The scan: a lossless 16-bit PNG in Display P3 wide color, to keep and to edit.
  • The DNG: exactly what the sensor recorded, kept beside every scan.
  • A JPEG when you share, for shops, social and email.

What it is not

  • It is not a document scanner. There is no text recognition.
  • It is for work up to letter size. A filled frame gives about 176 DPI in a 3 megapixel file: plenty for screens and small prints, and less than a flatbed.
  • It will not make your artwork look better than it is. That part is yours.
